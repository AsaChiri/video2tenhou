How this tool works

Review a question beside its video evidence and save the answer. Rebuild changes applies saved answers to the affected hands. With no saved changes waiting, Rebuild hand retries the selected hand's search.

A new video starts on Calibrate

The cameras move between broadcasts, so the first thing to do on a video is Calibrate: Measure table again measures the overhead from the table's centre unit and suggests the panels, Check borders saves changed regions and tests their borders. Drag incorrect regions into place, then check again. A region whose border cuts tiles makes every reading of it wrong, and no answer to a question here can repair that, so analysis waits until the check passes.

Question kinds

  • draw — inspect the player's hand before and after the draw, then identify the drawn tile.
  • discard — confirm the tile that entered the pond at the selected turn.
  • conflict — the reconstruction is impossible as read; the text names the candidates (usually a fifth copy of a tile). Fix the misread one.
  • result — compare the final hand with the video. Winning-tile, indicator and meld corrections are available in the collapsed controls.
  • kan: a concealed kan whose tile was not seen: correct the meld; or a kan with no time: click the discard that followed it.
  • riichi — sideways tiles and the site disagree on who declared riichi.
  • ura / dora — enter the indicator tiles seen in the overhead picture.
  • order — a note about turn order; acknowledge it or fix the discard it names.

Can't tell, where offered, marks that specific choice as unavailable in the footage; it does not confirm the guessed tile. Skip leaves the question for later.

Players are named by the seat wind of that hand (as on the broadcast overlay) and their nick, e.g. "East (zemzem)". Inside a hanchan the winds rotate with the dealer, so the same person is "East" in one hand and "North" in the next.

Tile names: 1m…9m characters, 1p…9p circles, 1s…9s bamboo, 1z…7z = 東 南 西 北 白 發 中, red fives 0m 0p 0s.
